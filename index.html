01 Introduction

Welcome to the Florida State Roleplay Community (FSRPC) Staff Team. As a staff member, you represent FSRPC and are expected to follow all community and staff rules. Your primary responsibility is to help players, enforce rules fairly, and maintain a positive roleplay environment.

Staff membership is a privilege, not a right. Staff may be suspended, demoted, or relieved of duty by FSRPC Management.

02 Trial Period

14
DAYSRequired trial period

New staff must complete Staff Training within 14 days of hire. During the trial period, Management evaluates professionalism, rule enforcement, clear communication, and integrity.

After successfully completing training and demonstrating the ability to represent FSRPC, trial staff may become a full Moderator.

03 Basic Staff Command Usage

ON-DUTY ONLY

Commands may only be used while On-Duty. HR+ may use commands Off-Duty for legitimate administrative purposes.

4+ CHARACTERS

Use at least four username characters for punitive actions. Spectating commands are exempt.

04 Staff Quota & Time Management

9
